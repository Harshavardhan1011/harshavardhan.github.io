I'm a first-year Electrical & Instrumentation Engineering student at BIT College, Bengaluru — balancing academics with a relentless creative practice built over 3+ years.

My work spans portrait and cinematic photography, short-form and long-form video editing, color science, motion graphics, and gaming highlight reels. Every frame I shoot or edit is a deliberate choice — not an accident.

Gaming sharpened my strategy, reflexes, and problem-solving. Photography trained my eye. Editing taught me patience. Engineering is teaching me precision.
